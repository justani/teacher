# Golden Tutoring Session: Area of a Circle Sector

## Problem

Find the area of a sector of a circle with radius 7 cm if the angle of the sector is 60°.

## Tutoring flow

**Me:** Okay, what is the question asking us to do?

**She:** Find the area of a sector of circle.

**Me:** do you know what is a sector of a circle?

**She:** Yes. [She draws it]

**Me:** nice, yes that is correct. Do you know how to find the area then?

**She:** i know the formula i think, it is mostly two pi r multiplied by theta divided by 360.

**Me:** nope

**She:** oh, i think it is pi r square multiplied by theta divided by 360.

**Me:** Perfect. What is r and theta then?

**She:** r is radius and theta is the angle which the sector makes.

**Me:** cool, then our expression becomes: [I draw 22 / 7 * 7 * 7 * 60 / 360 on the board]. Go ahead solve it.

**She:** So 7 and 7 gets cut

**Me:** [cut one 7 in numerator and another in denominator on the board]

**She:** then 22 * 7 is 144

**Me:** No, slight mistake. Middle number is wrong

**She:** Oh right, it is 154. Then we do 60 / 360, we can cut them and get 1 / 6

**Me:** [cuts 60 and 360 and replace with 1 and 6]. What do we have now?

**She:** now we have 154 / 6

**Me:** nice, looks good, can we simplify?

**She:** hmm, we can cut with 2

**Me:** go ahead

**She:** it will be 77 / 3

**Me:** let's convert to decimal.

**She:** okay, after dividing, i get twenty five point 6 bar.

**Me:** perfect, very nice. That is the right answer. Let's go to the next problem.
